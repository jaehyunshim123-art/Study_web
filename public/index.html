HOW TO PLAY

Your syllabus is now a wagon journey. Each unit is one leg of the trail, and the final exam is the finish line.

  1. 1

    Read the lesson

    Every unit starts with a short summary: an overview, key points and key terms. Reopen it from any question with REVIEW LESSON.

  2. 2

    Answer questions

    Pick A, B, C or D. Tap an answer, press the arcade buttons under the screen, or use keys 1-4.

  3. 3

    Earn coins and miles

    Right answers earn coins and move your wagon. 100 miles reaches the unit's landmark and opens the next lesson.

  4. 4

    Keep a streak

    Answers in a row build a streak. A wrong answer resets it and costs a few coins, and that question comes back later so you can learn it.

  5. 5

    Upgrade in the shop

    Spend coins on Earnings (more coins), Streak bonus (extra coins per streak) and Stronger oxen (more miles per answer).

  6. 6

    Reach the final exam

    Clear every unit to finish the trail and see your accuracy and best streak. EXIT saves your game anytime.
