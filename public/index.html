HOW TO PLAY

Your syllabus is now a wagon journey. Each unit is one leg of the trail, and the final exam is the finish line.

  1. 1

    Read the lesson

    Every unit starts with a short summary: an overview, key points and key terms. Reopen it from any question with REVIEW LESSON.

  2. 2

    Answer questions

    Pick A, B, C or D. Tap an answer, press the arcade buttons under the screen (A top, B right, C bottom, D left), or use keys 1-4.

  3. 3

    Earn coins and miles

    Right answers earn coins and move your wagon. 100 miles reaches the unit's landmark and opens the next lesson.

  4. 4

    Keep a streak

    Answers in a row build a streak. A wrong answer resets it and costs 1 coin (EASY questions) or 2 coins (HARD ones), and that question comes back later so you can learn it.

  5. 5

    Upgrade in the shop

    The store matches your subject (a law bookshop, a lab supply, a pharmacy...). Spend coins on upgrades that earn more coins per answer, pay extra for streaks, and give you stronger oxen (more miles per answer).

  6. 6

    Reach the final exam

    Clear every unit to finish the trail. EXIT saves your game anytime. Tap the clock on the control panel for a focus/break study timer.
